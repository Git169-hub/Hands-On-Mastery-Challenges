# Exercise 2 – Personalized AI Responses Using Context

## Objective

Understand how providing personal context improves Gemini's responses, even without using Custom Instructions.

---

## Prompt Used

```text
Act as my long-term AI career mentor.

Context:
- I am a final-year B.Tech Computer Science student specializing in AI & ML.
- I use Windows 11, VS Code, Python, GitHub and Google Colab.
- My goal is to become an AI/ML Engineer.
- I prefer beginner-friendly explanations with practical examples.
- Avoid unnecessary jargon.

Based on this information, create a 12-month roadmap to become an AI Engineer.
```

---

## Observation

My Gemini for Education account does not currently provide the Custom Instructions or Memory features.

Instead, I included my background, learning preferences, development environment, and career goals directly inside the prompt.

Gemini generated a personalized AI Engineering roadmap tailored to my profile.

---

## Key Learning

Adding detailed context inside prompts allows Gemini to produce responses that are more relevant, actionable, and personalized.

---

## Reflection

This exercise demonstrated that high-quality prompting is not only about asking the right question but also about supplying meaningful context. Even without Custom Instructions, Gemini adapts its recommendations effectively when sufficient information is provided.
